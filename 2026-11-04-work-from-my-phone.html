Could I do most of my work from my phone?

2026-11-04

A couple of weeks ago I asked my bot whether it could talk to the Codex and Claude on my own PC, or whether they had to be installed inside its VM. Then I wrote about which way messages should go between my agents. This morning I bookmarked a post from @davis7 that sounds like someone who already has this working.

He says: "Highly recommend connecting your t3 code machines to grok bot. I've been doing more and more work from my phone because of it."

And then the part I care about most: "Also works great with cursor cloud agents (esp the projects setup). With the cursor setup it's a two way thing where grok bot and cursor can message back and forth."

He's also honest that it isn't perfect yet: "T3 code isn't quite as seemless yet but it'll get there…"

Two way is the bit I was missing

When I thought about messages between agents before, I mostly pictured one direction. I tell a bot something, it goes off and does it. "Back and forth" is different. The coding agent can come back and ask, or report, and the bot that knows me can answer without waiting for me.

That changes what the phone is for. I wouldn't be typing code on a tiny screen. I'd be the person who checks in, reads what happened, and says yes or no. That actually sounds doable from a phone.

A smaller thing I saved

I also bookmarked @rileybrown saying he won't rely on an app to recommend podcasts anymore: "i'll just have my agent search thousands of episodes, transcripts included, and recommend what i should listen to." It's the same idea from a different side. Instead of opening an app and picking, you hand the picking to an agent and just look at what it brings back.

What I'd try

Connect my own machine, give a cloud agent one small real task, and see how often it needs me. If the answer is "a few short replies from my phone," then I've learned something. If it's "constantly," then the two way part isn't doing its job yet, and I'd want to know why.
